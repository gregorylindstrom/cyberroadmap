// Earn while you learn

No degree? No problem.

Registered apprenticeships flip the model: instead of funding school, an employer pays you a salary while training you into the role. In 2025, more than 58,000 registered apprentices were served across tech and cybersecurity programs. For career changers and community college students, this is often the fastest path in.

A word on service commitments, from the professor.

Students ask me whether the federal full-ride programs are "worth it." Here's how I frame it after 35 years in this field, much of it in and around government work: the service commitment isn't a cost — it's a guaranteed first job with a security clearance attached. A clearance is worth tens of thousands of dollars in salary premium and takes a year or more to get any other way. Graduating debt-free with a clearance and two years of federal experience is one of the strongest starting positions in this industry.

The honest trade-off: you give up flexibility. If you already know you want a startup or a specific company, the no-strings association scholarships and apprenticeships keep your options open. If you're unsure where you're headed — and most students are — the service programs are the best deal in American higher education right now.

One practical tip: the big federal programs require attending a designated NCAE-C school. Check whether your school qualifies before you fall in love with a program.
